Notes on a very strange cat+

Jeff used to be a regular cat. At some point, he became a button. Nobody knows why. Every new website you discover brings him a little closer to being a cat again.

Find them all and he’ll shake, vanish, and turn back. Your finds stay here. Add a new website to the pile and Jeff returns. Apparently, being a button is a recurring condition.

Progress is saved in this browser. No account, no tracking.

About Jeff

He’s a cat.
He’s also a button.

Press Jeff to open a website you haven’t found yet. Once you’ve found every destination, he turns back into a cat. Your finds and favorites stay here for return visits.

Each game and website belongs to its creators. Jeff links to them; he doesn’t copy or embed their work. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.

Discoveries, favorites, and preferences are saved only in this browser. No account or analytics. When browser storage is unavailable, progress lasts for the session.
